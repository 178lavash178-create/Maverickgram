Six runtime assets, unmodified upstream 64x64 PNG files:
chats.png      actions/view-conversation-balloon
contacts.png   apps/system-users
calls.png      apps/internet-telephony
workshop.png   categories/preferences-system
settings.png   categories/applications-system
downloads.png  actions/go-down

These images are drawn at a logical 32-point size. Original 64-pixel detail
is retained for high-resolution displays. The original 32x32 PNGs are in
original-32/ for provenance; the runtime uses the 64x64 originals consistently.
In particular, Oxygen's 32px contacts design is a blue badge while its 64px
source is the two-person artwork selected for this toolbar.

Original editable sources are supplied, unchanged and gzip-compressed, in
source/*.svgz. No icons were redrawn, generated, recolored or rasterized for
this asset import. AppKit may scale/tint the supplied artwork at runtime.
